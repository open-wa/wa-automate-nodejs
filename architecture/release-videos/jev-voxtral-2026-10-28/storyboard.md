## Production notes

The canonical URL stays visible in the header throughout. Titles and messages explain the film without sound. Original procedural pads, swells and chimes follow the chapter boundaries; there is no narration or sampled music. The final card settles within one second and remains readable for over ten seconds.

Calling and rich-message prerequisites appear on the final card. Booking scenes describe chat confirmations, not external reservations. The recovery scene specifically covers an answer/TTS request failing while transcription stays open. The illustrated interface is labelled in the footer.
