# ✨ OpenWA 5.3.0 — your session travels light

One encrypted JSON file. A lot less baggage when you move your session!

## ✨ What's new

- 🔒️ **Compact auth is the default.** `{sessionId}.data.json` replaces the persistent browser-profile folder, with encrypted `auth` and `data` payloads.
- 📨 **Pick up pending messages.** Replay through `onMessage` and acknowledge after your handlers finish. Retries are at least once, so deduplicate side effects.
- ♻️ **Keep your storage setup.** Existing filenames, paths, seeds and S3 settings still work through the same PicoS3 transport.
- 🔒️ **Cleaner diagnostics.** Session payloads and S3 credentials are redacted from debug responses.

## 🚚 Upgrading

Pair once for compact auth, or set `legacyDataDirAuth: true` to keep your existing browser profile. There's no `portableSession` option or key-provider setup.

The adapter is WhatsApp-build-gated. Live browser/S3 acceptance and full crash recovery remain unverified.
