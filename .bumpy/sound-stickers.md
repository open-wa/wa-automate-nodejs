✨ Send stickers that play a sound. `sendSoundSticker(to, sticker, sound)` takes any image and a short Ogg Opus or M4A clip (up to 1 MB) and sends them as one sound sticker; the recipient taps it to hear the clip. It needs no license and works over the SDK, the socket client and `POST /api/messages/sendSoundSticker`.
